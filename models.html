Silhouette is the score that matters: the finished mesh is sampled, projected through the measured camera into all 36 views, and compared with each photograph's own outline. It measures the published object, not the voxel grid it came from — smoothing and decimation happen after the carve and both move the outline. H / W error is against a tape: 270 mm tall, 115 mm at the widest. Edge bulge is how much too fat the model is seen end-on, where a turntable has fewest views to constrain it. Genus counts holes through the surface — a trigger sprayer really has one, so 0 or 1 is right and 24 means the mesher broke. Anything not watertight, or more than 8% off the tape, is rejected rather than published.
