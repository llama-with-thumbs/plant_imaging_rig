How this was made, and what it is not.
  • The turntable angle is known to a fraction of a degree, so no feature matching was needed — each photograph’s silhouette carves away everything it rules out, and what survives all 36 is the model.
  • A silhouette method cannot see concavities. The dish behind the trigger and the waist under the label come out filled. That is inherent to the technique.
  • The camera geometry is fitted, not assumed: where the rotation axis falls in the image, how far the camera tilts down (−15.8°), and how strong the perspective is (a camera 431 mm away) were each solved by carving from half the views and scoring how well the result predicted the other half.
  • That raised agreement with the photographs from an IoU of 0.78 to 0.83. What remains is mostly lens distortion, which a checkerboard calibration would remove.
